  In my introductory Electrical Engineering class, we had a handful of labs that had us applying our knowledge from lecture on breadboard projects. One series of these such projects involved a four-part series of:

  • working up from a simple 3x3 led matrix and using an IDE to program an ESP32 driver
  • creating a microphone with high-pass and low-pass filters
  • soldering up an 8x8 LED board with the previously-created microphone and microcontroller
  • then finally putting together professional parts (higher-quality microphone, ESP32 and a 32x128 LED board) as the capstone to this series of labs.
